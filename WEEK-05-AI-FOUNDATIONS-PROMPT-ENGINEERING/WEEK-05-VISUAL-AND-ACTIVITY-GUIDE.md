## Screenshot and privacy standards
- Capture only from instructor-controlled, approved accounts.
- Use fictional prompts and data.
- Hide account names, email addresses, chat history, notifications and other private details.
- Do not show API keys, passwords, access tokens or personal records.
- Add a caption and alt text.
- Record source and licence for external images.
- Product interfaces change; date screenshots and refresh them when they become misleading.
- If a tool is unavailable, use a teacher-created mock example or printed worksheet. Do not fabricate a screenshot and label it as a real product interface.

## Instructor preparation
- Check current regional availability, age rules, account requirements and free-tier limits before class.
- Prepare offline examples so the lesson can continue if internet access fails.
- Use identical harmless prompts for comparison activities.
- Prepare a reliable source for students to verify factual claims.
