# Week 7 — Student Quiz Bank
## AI for Graphic Design, Video and Digital Content

**Instructions:** Answer in your own words. For multiple-choice questions, select one best answer. Do not use private personal data in examples.

### Lesson 31 — Image generation
1. What does a text-to-image system do?
2. Which prompt element explains what the image is for?  
   A. Purpose  B. File extension  C. Password  D. Email address
3. Name four useful elements of an image prompt.
4. Why should important words be added in a design editor rather than trusted to generated image text?
5. Give three visual details that should be checked in a generated image.
6. True or false: A realistic AI image proves that the event shown really happened.

### Lesson 32 — Posters, thumbnails and social design
7. What is visual hierarchy?
8. Which thumbnail is usually easier to understand on a phone?  
   A. One focal point and a few readable words  B. Ten paragraphs  C. Many unrelated pictures  D. Tiny low-contrast text
9. Name four design principles.
10. Why must poster dates, locations and contact details be checked manually?
11. What is the benefit of saving an editable project file?
12. Explain why a thumbnail should accurately represent the video.

### Lesson 33 — Channel planning
13. What is the purpose of defining a target audience?
14. What are content pillars?
15. Which title is more responsible?  
   A. “Guaranteed Secret That Makes Everyone Rich”  
   B. “How to Organise Files in Windows: A Beginner Guide”
16. What should a simple educational video outline contain?
17. Name three things to check before publishing a video that includes other people's media.
18. Why are views alone not proof of educational quality?

### Lesson 34 — Video and Shorts
19. What is a storyboard?
20. Put these steps in a sensible order: edit, plan, review, script/storyboard, export.
21. What is the purpose of a hook?
22. Why should current platform specifications be checked before exporting?
23. Name four final video quality checks.
24. Give one valid non-AI way to create a short educational video.

### Lesson 35 — Audio, voice and captions
25. What is the difference between text-to-speech and speech-to-text?
26. Why must automatic captions be reviewed?
27. What is voice cloning?
28. Why is consent important when using another person's voice?
29. What is a deepfake?
30. Name four checks for a responsible audio-video publication.

### Application questions
31. You generate an image for a school poster, but the tool misspells the academy name. What should you do?
32. A thumbnail promises “100% guaranteed success” but the video only gives general advice. What is wrong and how would you fix it?
33. An AI tool suggests a famous song for your video. What must you check before using it?
34. A student wants to clone a teacher's voice for a joke. Explain why this is not acceptable without explicit permission.
35. An automatic caption changes a technical word and makes the lesson incorrect. What should happen before publishing?
