# Week 7 — AI for Graphic Design, Video and Digital Content

**Course:** Computer Fundamentals and Artificial Intelligence Foundation Course  
**Academy:** Next Generation Academy  
**Duration:** 5 lessons / 10 guided hours  
**Level:** Beginner  
**Teaching approach:** Approximately 20% explanation and 80% guided/independent practice

## Week purpose
Students learn to plan and create educational graphics and short-form media. They practise AI image prompting, poster and thumbnail design, channel planning, short-video production, audio and captions. Responsible use, copyright, privacy, consent and accessibility are taught throughout.

## Suggested weekly delivery
- Day 1: Image generation and visual quality (Lesson 31)
- Day 2: Posters, thumbnails and social media (Lesson 32)
- Day 3: Channel planning and content strategy (Lesson 33)
- Day 4: Short-video scripting, storyboarding and editing (Lesson 34)
- Day 5: Audio, captions, responsible production and assessment (Lesson 35)

Each lesson uses a 120-minute structure: 20 minutes of explanation, 90 minutes of demonstration and practical work, and 10 minutes of review.

## Equipment and preparation
- Computers with a current browser and a suitable image/design editor.
- Optional phone/camera, microphone or headset, headphones and video editor.
- Instructor-prepared sample images, scripts, storyboard and fictional content.
- Offline or conventional alternatives for students without access to AI generation tools.
- Check tool age requirements, current availability, pricing, privacy terms and export rights before class. No paid account is compulsory.

## Responsible-use requirements
- Use fictional or approved material; never upload private student information.
- Obtain appropriate consent before recording or publishing identifiable people.
- Do not clone or imitate another person's voice without explicit permission.
- Check licences and attribution requirements for images, music, video, fonts and templates.
- Verify facts and captions; do not publish misleading synthetic media.
- Obtain academy approval before any public posting.

## Completion evidence
Students should save their prompt/design notes, at least two reviewed visual designs, channel plan, script/storyboard, short video or complete prototype, captions/transcript, source notes and responsible-use reflection.
