## A. Written quiz — 15 marks
Select questions across all five lessons. Suggested allocation:
- Image generation and prompt writing: 3
- Design principles and formats: 3
- Channel strategy and publishing: 3
- Video planning and editing: 3
- Audio, captions, consent and safety: 3

Mark for understanding, not memorised wording. Use the instructor-only answer key.

| Criterion | Marks | Evidence |

These are internal course guidance bands, not an external certification standard.
