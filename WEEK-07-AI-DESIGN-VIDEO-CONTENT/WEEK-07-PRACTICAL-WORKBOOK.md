## Responsible publishing checklist
- [ ] Facts and claims checked against reliable sources.
- [ ] Music, images, video and fonts have suitable permissions/licences.
- [ ] People shown or heard have given appropriate consent.
- [ ] No student records, passwords, private messages or contact details are visible.
- [ ] AI-generated or synthetic media is not presented deceptively.
- [ ] Captions, contrast and text size support accessibility.
- [ ] Platform rules and current format requirements checked.
- [ ] Instructor approval obtained before public posting.
