# Week 3 — Windows Management, Installation, Internet and Networking

**Lessons:** 11–15 | **Duration:** 5 lessons / 10 guided hours  
**Course:** Computer Fundamentals and Artificial Intelligence Foundation Course  
**Academy:** Next Generation Academy

## Learning purpose
Students learn to inspect Windows safely, understand storage layout, recognize the stages of Windows installation, explain basic networks, use browsers and email professionally, and recognize phishing attempts.

## Standard lesson delivery
Each lesson is planned for 120 minutes: approximately 20 minutes of theory, 90 minutes of practical demonstration and practice, and 10 minutes of review. Adapt pacing to student readiness while preserving supervised practice and safety.

## Required equipment and preparation
- Windows training computers with standard student accounts.
- Projector or large display.
- Instructor-controlled spare computer or disposable virtual machine for installation demonstration.
- Approved Windows installation media and valid licensing appropriate to the training device.
- Sample storage layout or instructor-controlled test machine.
- Router/network equipment or safe diagrams; approved internet access.
- Browser and instructor-controlled email test account.
- Printed workbooks and assessment records.

## Critical safety and privacy rules
- Disk Management activities are view-only for students. No formatting, deletion, initialization, resizing or conversion.
- Windows installation must use a disposable VM or dedicated spare training PC. Never use a student's everyday device.
- Verify backups, target device, licence, power and recovery plan before a controlled installation.
- Do not install drivers from unknown sites or use unapproved driver-updater tools.
- Students may inspect only academy-approved networks. Do not share Wi-Fi passwords or private network information.
- Do not display real student inboxes or personal accounts. Use fictional phishing examples and instructor-controlled test accounts.
- Never share passwords or one-time verification codes.

## Visual status
Conceptual diagrams are embedded in all five lesson files. The visual guide specifies genuine screenshots and equipment photographs to capture at the academy. No fabricated Windows screenshots are used.

## Completion outcomes
By the end of Week 3, students should be able to inspect basic system activity and device status, explain storage concepts, describe a safe Windows installation workflow, explain a simple network, use a browser and email appropriately, and identify common phishing warning signs.
