## Written quiz — 20 marks
Select 20 questions from the Week 3 Quiz Bank. Suggested balance: Lesson 11: 4 marks; Lesson 12: 4; Lesson 13: 4; Lesson 14: 4; Lesson 15: 4. Use the separate instructor-only answer key. Accept equivalent correct explanations.

## Homework and participation — 10 marks
- Workbook completed with useful observations: 4
- Homework quality and accuracy: 3
- Safe conduct, teamwork and participation: 2
- Reflection and asking for help appropriately: 1

## Critical safety expectations
A student must not receive full practical credit if they attempt to format/delete/convert a disk, install unapproved drivers, access an unauthorized network, share credentials, or continue a destructive installation step without permission. Stop the activity, explain the risk, and record the incident according to academy policy. Do not shame the student; reteach and supervise.
