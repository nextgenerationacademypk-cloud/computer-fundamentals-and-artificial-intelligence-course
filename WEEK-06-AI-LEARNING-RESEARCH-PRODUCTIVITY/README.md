# Week 6 — AI for Learning, Research and Office Productivity

**Lessons:** 26–30 · **Guided learning time:** 10 hours  
**Course week:** 6 of 8

## Week purpose
Students learn to use AI as a responsible assistant for research, professional writing, spreadsheets and presentations. They practise checking sources, citations, facts, formulas and exported files. The goal is not simply to generate content; students must understand, review and take responsibility for their work.

## Learning outcomes
By the end of this week, students can:
- Create a source-grounded research notebook and verify citations.
- Use AI to plan and improve Word documents, PDFs and email drafts.
- Ask for spreadsheet formula help and test the results.
- Create and improve an AI-assisted presentation.
- Combine multiple office deliverables into one organized workflow.
- Protect private information and explain how AI assisted their work.

## Required resources
Computer and browser; approved public or teacher-created source documents; Microsoft Word/Excel/PowerPoint where licensed or LibreOffice Writer/Calc/Impress; internet access for online AI tools where available; fictional datasets and sample documents.

## Tool access and alternatives
NotebookLM and Gamma are examples of tools, not mandatory paid requirements. Their features, account eligibility, free limits, supported file types and export options may change. The instructor must check access and terms before the course. Provide equivalent offline or office-suite alternatives so learning outcomes do not depend on paid subscriptions, student accounts or uninterrupted internet.

## Privacy and responsible use
- Use fictional or public learning data in exercises.
- Never upload private student records, passwords, identity documents or confidential academy files to public AI tools.
- Verify citations, factual claims, formulas, calculations and generated images.
- Do not send emails or publish files without human review and explicit approval.
- Follow copyright, licence, attribution and academy academic-integrity policies.

## Assessment
Week 6 assessment is out of 50 marks: quiz/oral understanding (10), practical tasks (25), integrated office package (10), responsible AI use and reflection (5). A generated output alone is not evidence of mastery; students must explain and verify it.

## Completion standard
Students should complete the workbook activities, submit the integrated office package, explain at least one AI-assisted decision and demonstrate source, formula, privacy and file-quality checks.
