## A. Written quiz — 10 marks
Select ten questions from the Week 6 quiz bank. One mark each. Include at least:
- Two source/citation questions.
- Two document/PDF questions.
- Two spreadsheet questions.
- Two presentation questions.
- Two workflow/privacy questions.

| Criterion | Marks |
|---|---:|

## Performance guidance
- **45–50:** Strong — accurate work and independent verification.
- **38–44:** Good — minor errors; checks are mostly sound.
- **30–37:** Developing — needs some support with verification or file quality.
- **Below 30:** Further practice and reassessment recommended.

A student should not pass a practical skill solely because an AI tool generated an output. The student must explain and demonstrate the checking process.

## Instructor notes
- Record tool access issues separately from student skill gaps.
- Provide equivalent offline tasks where accounts, connectivity or product limits prevent use.
- Never place real student marks or sensitive records in a public AI tool for assessment.
- Keep answer keys and completed student records in instructor-controlled storage.
