# Week 6 — Student Practical Workbook

**AI for Learning, Research and Office Productivity**  
**Lessons 26–30 · Total guided time: 10 hours**

## Student instructions
- Use only teacher-approved sources and fictional practice data.
- Do not enter passwords, private student details, identity numbers, private emails or confidential academy records into AI tools.
- Check AI output before using it.
- If an online tool is unavailable, complete the stated offline alternative.
- Save your work in the course folder using clear file names.
