# Week 6 — Visual and Screenshot Guide

## Purpose
This guide identifies where visuals will improve learning and how the instructor can capture accurate, current screenshots. Product interfaces change frequently. Use genuine screenshots from the version installed or accessible at the academy; do not present fabricated screens as real product interfaces.

## Screenshot capture checklist
- [ ] Capture from the actual product and current interface.
- [ ] Use a teacher-created demo account or approved public sample.
- [ ] Remove personal names, email addresses, account avatars and private documents.
- [ ] Do not show API keys, passwords, browser profile details or private tabs.
- [ ] Use fictional data in all workbook screenshots.
- [ ] Keep text large enough to read at normal zoom.
- [ ] Crop irrelevant browser chrome without hiding important controls.
- [ ] Add a short caption explaining what the student should notice.
- [ ] Add alternative text when the repository page or document format supports it.
- [ ] Record product name and capture date because interfaces can change.

## Diagram and image standards
- Prefer simple diagrams with clear labels and a single learning purpose.
- Keep colours high-contrast and avoid relying on colour alone.
- Use landscape 16:9 screenshots where practical.
- Do not use copyrighted textbook or paid-course screenshots without permission.
- Credit third-party images and follow the licence terms.
- Use SVG or Mermaid for conceptual diagrams where possible; use screenshots only when showing an actual interface.
