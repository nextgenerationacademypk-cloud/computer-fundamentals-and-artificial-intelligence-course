## Week 1 learning outcomes

By the end of this week, students should be able to:
- Explain what computers do and describe the IPOS cycle.
- Recognize major milestones in computer history and the commonly taught generations.
- Identify different types of computers and their uses.
- Distinguish hardware, system software, and application software.
- Identify the main internal components of a desktop and follow basic hardware safety rules.

## Practical equipment

- Desktop computer or laptop for demonstrations.
- Spare training desktop with instructor-approved internal access.
- Labelled component diagram or component cards.
- Keyboard, mouse, monitor, printer, USB storage device, and other available peripherals.
- ESD protection and suitable tools for supervised hardware practice.

## Safety requirements

Students must never open a power supply unit, work inside a powered computer, force a component, or open a personal computer without permission and direct supervision. Use a spare training desktop for internal inspection.
