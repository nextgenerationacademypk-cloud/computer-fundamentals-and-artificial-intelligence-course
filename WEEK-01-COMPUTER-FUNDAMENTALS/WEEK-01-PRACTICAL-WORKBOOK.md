## Practical 6 — System unit identification
**Goal:** Identify major internal components safely.

Use only an instructor-approved spare desktop or labelled component samples. Do not open a personal computer. Never open a power supply unit (PSU).

1. Observe the outside of the system unit.
2. Identify available external ports.
3. Watch the instructor shut down and disconnect the training desktop.
4. Follow the lab's ESD procedure.
5. Identify the motherboard, CPU cooling assembly, RAM, storage, PSU casing, GPU (if present), network interface, fans, and CMOS battery.
6. Record observations. Do not remove parts unless the instructor specifically authorizes and supervises the task.
