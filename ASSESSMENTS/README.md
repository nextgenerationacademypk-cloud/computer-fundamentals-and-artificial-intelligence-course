# Assessments and Records

This folder contains the course-level assessment framework and printable assessment instruments. Weekly quizzes, instructor-only answer keys, workbooks and 50-mark weekly assessment records remain in their respective week folders.

## Course-level assessment resources

- [Course Assessment Framework and Marking Guide](COURSE-ASSESSMENT-FRAMEWORK-AND-MARKING-GUIDE.md) — approved 100-mark framework, assessment principles, mid-course and final project rubrics, feedback and moderation.
- [Mid-Course Practical Examination](MID-COURSE-PRACTICAL-EXAMINATION.md) — student instructions, six practical tasks, 15-mark marking sheet and contingency guidance.
- [Student Progress, Attendance and Completion Record](STUDENT-PROGRESS-ATTENDANCE-AND-COMPLETION-RECORD.md) — confidential student record template for attendance, weekly progress, skills, marks and completion.

## Approved course assessment

| Component | Marks |
|---|---:|
| Weekly practical activities | 30 |
| Homework and assignments | 15 |
| Mid-course practical examination | 15 |
| Final project and presentation | 30 |
| Theory, oral assessment and participation | 10 |
| **Total** | **100** |

Recommended pass: 60/100, together with final project submission/presentation and basic independent computer skills. Weekly 50-mark assessments are formative evidence; do not add them together as a separate course total.

## Record privacy

Completed student records contain personal educational information. Keep them in an academy-approved private, access-controlled location. Never upload real student marks, attendance, contact details or individual feedback to this public repository or an AI tool.
