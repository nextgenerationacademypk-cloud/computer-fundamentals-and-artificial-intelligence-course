# Student Orientation Handbook

**Computer Fundamentals and Artificial Intelligence Foundation Course**  
**Next Generation Academy** | Seni Gumbat, Kohat, Pakistan

Welcome! This course will help you use computers confidently, create useful digital work, understand artificial intelligence (AI), and publish a simple website.

## 1. Your course at a glance

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Duration | 8 weeks |
| Lessons | 40 |
| Guided learning | 80 hours |
| Weekly schedule | 5 lessons |
| Lesson length | 2 hours |
| Level | Beginner |
| Language | Simple English |
| Learning style | About 80% practical work and 20% explanation |

You do not need programming experience. You should be ready to practise, ask questions, work respectfully with others and complete learning tasks.

## 2. What you will learn

1. **Week 1:** Computer basics and hardware.
2. **Week 2:** Windows, settings, files and folders.
3. **Week 3:** Computer management, safe installation concepts, internet and networks.
4. **Week 4:** Word, Excel, PowerPoint and digital productivity.
5. **Week 5:** AI basics and professional prompts.
6. **Week 6:** AI for research, documents, spreadsheets and presentations.
7. **Week 7:** AI images, graphic design, video and digital content.
8. **Week 8:** AI-assisted website building, GitHub and publishing.

## 3. How to learn in every lesson

Most lessons follow this pattern:
1. Listen to a short explanation.
2. Watch the instructor demonstrate.
3. Follow the steps with help.
4. Complete a task yourself.
5. Show your work and explain what you did.
6. Review mistakes and save your work.

Do not worry if you need more than one attempt. Asking for help is part of learning.

## 4. What to bring and prepare

- A notebook and pen.
- Your course workbook or printed lesson sheet.
- A USB drive only if the instructor asks you to bring one.
- Your own laptop only if the academy has approved it.

Do not bring unapproved software, unknown USB devices or personal files containing private information for classroom demonstrations.

## 5. How to save your course work

Use the folder structure given by your instructor. A suggested structure is:

```text
Computer-AI-Course/
├── Week-01-Computer-Fundamentals/
├── Week-02-Windows/
├── Week-03-Internet-Networking/
├── Week-04-Office/
├── Week-05-AI-Prompts/
├── Week-06-AI-Productivity/
├── Week-07-AI-Content/
└── Week-08-Website-GitHub/
```

Use clear file names, for example `W04-Excel-Student-Marks-Practice.xlsx`. Save regularly. If you are unsure where to save, ask before closing the program. Keep a second copy in the academy-approved backup location when instructed.

## 7. Classroom expectations

- Arrive on time and follow the timetable.
- Listen when someone is explaining or demonstrating.
- Take turns and allow every group member to practise.
- Respect classmates, instructors and equipment.
- Ask before changing settings, installing software or connecting a device.
- Save your work and keep your files organised.
- Report damaged equipment, strange messages or mistakes immediately.
- Do not copy another student's work or pretend you completed a task you did not do.

## 8. Safety rules you must remember

- Keep food and drinks away from computers.
- Never open a computer case unless the instructor has approved and supervised the activity.
- Never open a power supply unit.
- Never format, delete, initialize or change a disk during student practice.
- Windows installation practice is only on an approved virtual machine or spare training computer under direct supervision.
- Never share passwords or verification codes.
- Never enter private student information into an AI tool.
- Do not click suspicious links or open unexpected attachments.

## 9. Using AI honestly

AI can help you understand a topic, improve a prompt, create a draft or explain a formula. It can also make mistakes or invent information.

Always:
- Check important facts.
- Test formulas and code.
- Read and edit AI-generated work.
- Use sources you can open and verify.
- Tell your instructor when AI helped with an assignment if asked.
- Make sure you can explain the work you submit.

AI is a learning helper. You are responsible for your final work.

## 10. Getting help

If you are stuck:
1. Read the instruction again.
2. Check the last step you completed.
3. Ask a nearby classmate to explain, not to do the task for you.
4. Ask the instructor.
5. Show the exact message or problem if possible.

Never guess when an action may delete files, change security, affect another person's account or damage equipment.
