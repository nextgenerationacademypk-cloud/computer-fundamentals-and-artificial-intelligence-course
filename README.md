# Computer Fundamentals and Artificial Intelligence Foundation Course

**Next Generation Academy**  
Seni Gumbat, Kohat, Pakistan

A practical, beginner-friendly 8-week programme that develops computer confidence, digital productivity, responsible AI use, and introductory AI-assisted website publishing skills.

## Learning pathway

1. Computer fundamentals and hardware
2. Windows and everyday computer operations
3. Windows management, installation, internet and networking
4. Microsoft Office and digital productivity
5. AI foundations and professional prompt engineering
6. AI for learning, research and office productivity
7. AI for graphic design, video and digital content
8. AI-assisted website development, GitHub and career readiness

## Standard lesson package

Each lesson is intended to include:
- Student lecture notes in simple English
- Measurable learning objectives and key vocabulary
- Instructor preparation and delivery notes
- Demonstration and guided practical work
- Independent student practice
- Homework or an extension task
- A short quiz and instructor-only answer key
- Assessment evidence and relevant visual resources

Instructor-only answer keys and assessment guidance must be clearly labelled and kept separate from student-facing material wherever practical.

## Safety and responsible use

- Hardware activities require instructor supervision. Disconnect power before opening a desktop case. Never open a power supply unit (PSU), and never force a component into a slot.
- Windows installation and partition activities must use a spare training computer or virtual machine. Back up data first; destructive actions require direct instructor supervision.
- Use legitimate software and official downloads. Do not share passwords, recovery codes, private student records or confidential information with AI tools.
- AI-generated information, documents, images and code must be checked before use. Students must disclose meaningful AI assistance and must not submit fabricated sources or plagiarised work.
- Tool interfaces, pricing and availability can change. Instructors should verify current access before each course delivery and prepare alternatives.
