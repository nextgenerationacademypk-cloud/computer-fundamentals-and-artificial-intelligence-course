# Instructor Guide

This folder contains the instructor-facing standards and practical tools for delivering the approved **Computer Fundamentals and Artificial Intelligence Foundation Course** consistently, safely and fairly.

## Main document

- [Instructor Manual — Course Delivery, Safety and Assessment](INSTRUCTOR-MANUAL.md)

## Manual contents

The instructor manual covers:
- Instructor responsibilities and the approved 8-week / 40-lesson delivery model.
- The standard 120-minute lesson structure and practical-first teaching cycle.
- Student readiness, orientation, classroom management and flexible grouping.
- Differentiation for beginners and students who need additional practice.
- Computer lab setup, device/account management and start/end routines.
- Hardware handling, electrical safety and incident response.
- Windows, storage, partition and installation safety.
- Internet, email, network and phishing safety.
- AI account eligibility, privacy, responsible use, Prompt Coach and synthetic media.
- Software licensing and offline alternatives.
- The approved course assessment framework, mid-course exam and final project rubric.
- Student progress tracking, remediation, troubleshooting and record keeping.
- Weekly, daily and end-of-course preparation checklists.
- Course policies and instructor sign-off.

## Important operating rules

1. Use the approved master curriculum and the corresponding lesson files.
2. Keep instructor answer keys and confidential student records separate from student-facing materials.
3. Student Disk Management activities are view-only.
4. Windows installation practice is restricted to a disposable virtual machine or dedicated spare training computer under direct instructor supervision.
5. Never open a PSU or allow students to work on live internal components.
6. Use fictional data for AI, email and spreadsheet demonstrations; never enter passwords, verification codes or private student records into AI tools.
7. Do not claim a course has been classroom-piloted or externally accredited until the relevant work has actually occurred.
