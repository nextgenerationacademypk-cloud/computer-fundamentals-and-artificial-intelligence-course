## Practical file review checklist
- [ ] Word file opens and remains editable.
- [ ] PDF export is readable and has no unexpected blank pages.
- [ ] Excel formulas produce plausible results and are not replaced by typed totals.
- [ ] Sorting preserved each record's relationship across columns.
- [ ] Chart title and labels are clear.
- [ ] PowerPoint is readable in Slide Show mode.
- [ ] Images and facts have appropriate source/permission information.
- [ ] Only fictional or approved data is used.
