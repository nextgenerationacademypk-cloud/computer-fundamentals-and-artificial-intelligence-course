# Week 4 Quiz Bank — Student Copy

**Lessons 16–20 | Suggested time:** 35–45 minutes

## Lesson 16 — Word Fundamentals
1. What is a word processor?
2. What is the purpose of the Ribbon?
3. What is the difference between Save and Save As?
4. What does Undo do?
5. When should you use a page break?
6. What is the difference between DOCX and PDF?
7. Name two paragraph alignment options.
8. Why should you use paragraph spacing instead of repeated blank lines?
9. Does spellcheck guarantee that a document is factually correct?
10. Why should you reopen a saved file?

## Lesson 17 — Professional Word Documents
1. What is a document style?
2. Why use Heading 1 and Heading 2?
3. What does a table of contents use to organize entries?
4. When is a table useful?
5. What is alternative text?
6. Why should images keep their proportions?
7. What is the purpose of a header or footer?
8. What is a hyperlink?
9. Give one feature of an accessible document.
10. Why should PDF output be inspected before sharing?

3. How is a transition different from an animation?

5. What are speaker notes?

## Mixed application
1. A Word document looks correct on screen but its PDF has a blank final page. What should you inspect?
2. An Excel table contains names and marks. What could go wrong if you sort only the marks column?
3. A copied formula changes a cell reference that should stay fixed. What type of reference may be needed?
4. A presentation uses many animations and tiny text. Suggest three improvements.
5. You find an attractive image online with no clear reuse licence. What should you do?

## Short practical assessment
- Create and export a Word document.
- Format a structured Excel table and filter it.
- Write and copy a SUM, AVERAGE and IF formula.
- Create a chart from a small dataset.
- Present three to five slides and identify the source of one visual.
