# Week 4 — Microsoft Office and Digital Productivity

**Lessons:** 16–20 | **Duration:** 5 lessons / 10 guided hours  
**Course:** Computer Fundamentals and Artificial Intelligence Foundation Course  
**Academy:** Next Generation Academy

## Learning purpose
Students build practical skills in Word, Excel and PowerPoint. They create professional documents, organize and analyze fictional spreadsheet data, prepare presentations and practise responsible digital content use.

## Standard lesson delivery
Each lesson is planned for 120 minutes: approximately 20 minutes theory, 90 minutes demonstration and hands-on practice, and 10 minutes review. Keep students working in the applications rather than watching long demonstrations.

## Required equipment and software
- Lab computers with Microsoft Word, Excel and PowerPoint where licensed.
- LibreOffice Writer, Calc and Impress as practical alternatives when Microsoft Office is unavailable.
- Projector/display, keyboard and mouse for each student or pair.
- Academy practice folder and approved storage/backup location.
- Instructor-approved sample documents, fictional spreadsheet data and permitted images.
- Printer/PDF export capability where available.

## Privacy and responsible-use rules
- Use fictional sample names, marks, attendance and expenses in demonstrations.
- Do not upload real student records or confidential academy documents to public tools or unapproved cloud services.
- Students must check facts, formulas, calculations and AI-assisted content.
- Images and other creative works must be self-created or reused under a suitable permission/licence, with attribution where required.
- Keep editable source files as well as exported PDFs when appropriate.

## Visual learning
A conceptual workflow diagram is embedded in each lesson. The visual guide describes genuine application screenshots to capture from the academy's installed software. Do not fabricate screenshots or expose private account information.

## Completion outcomes
Students should be able to create and export professional Word documents, build and filter Excel tables, use core formulas and charts, prepare and present clear PowerPoint slides, and apply basic copyright, attribution and privacy principles.
